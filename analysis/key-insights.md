# Key Analytical Findings & Strategic Insights

This document synthesizes the primary analytical conclusions verified directly from the Tableau workbook screenshots and the recovered 311-record dataset.

---

## 1. Production Operations Represents the Primary Attrition Bottleneck

* **Finding**: The Production department accounts for **67.2% of the workforce (209 employees)** and **79.8% of all organizational terminations (83 exits)**.
* **Context**: Production turnover sits at **39.71%**, compared to 20.00% in IT/IS and 16.13% in Sales.
* **Visual Evidence**: Confirmed via [Sheet 3](../screenshots/sheets/sheet-03-department-distribution.png) and [Dashboard 1](../dashboards/dashboard-1.png).

---

## 2. External Job Offers & Compensation Drive Voluntary Exits

* **Finding**: The leading stated reasons for voluntary termination are **Another position (20 employees)**, **Unhappy (14 employees)**, and **More money (11 employees)**.
* **Insight**: Combined, market mobility and pay dissatisfaction represent over **51% of voluntary departures**.
* **Visual Evidence**: Confirmed via [Sheet 5](../screenshots/sheets/sheet-05-termination-reasons.png) and [Dashboard 2](../dashboards/dashboard-2.png).

---

## 3. High Sourcing Concentration in Digital Job Boards

* **Finding**: **Indeed (87)** and **LinkedIn (76)** account for **52.4% of total recruitment volume**.
* **Insight**: While digital job boards drive high top-of-funnel applicant volume, **Employee Referrals (31 hires)** and **Diversity Job Fairs (29 hires)** demonstrate strong retention and gender balance.
* **Visual Evidence**: Confirmed via [Sheet 7](../screenshots/sheets/sheet-07-source-of-hire.png), [Sheet 15](../screenshots/sheets/sheet-15-diversity-recruitment-sources.png), and [Dashboard 3](../dashboards/dashboard-3.png).

---

## 4. Performance Ratings Follow a Healthy Normal Distribution

* **Finding**:
  * **Fully Meets**: 243 employees (**78.14%**)
  * **Exceeds**: 37 employees (**11.90%**)
  * **Needs Improvement**: 18 employees (**5.79%**)
  * **PIP**: 13 employees (**4.18%**)
* **Visual Evidence**: Confirmed via [Sheet 9](../screenshots/sheets/sheet-09-performance-scores.png) and [Sheet 10](../screenshots/sheets/sheet-10-leaderboard-top-performers.png).

---

## 5. Special Project Allocation is Heavily Siloed

* **Finding**: Of the 379 total special project assignments recorded in the organization, **IT/IS completed 296 (78.1%)** and **Software Engineering completed 46 (12.1%)**, while the 209 Production staff received only **4 special projects combined**.
* **Insight**: Frontline production employees have virtually zero access to stretch assignments or cross-functional projects, contributing to voluntary exits for alternative career paths.
* **Visual Evidence**: Confirmed via [Sheet 12](../screenshots/sheets/sheet-12-feedback-analysis.png) and [Sheet 11](../screenshots/sheets/sheet-11-satisfaction-scores.png).
