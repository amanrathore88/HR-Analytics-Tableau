# Strategic Business Recommendations

Based on the verified findings from the 311-employee HR dataset, the following data-backed recommendations are structured for People Operations and Talent Leadership:

---

## 1. Targeted Retention in Production Operations

* **Verified Finding**: The Production department accounts for **67.2% of total headcount (209 employees)** and represents the vast majority of voluntary departures. The top three verified voluntary termination reasons are:
  1. *Another position* (20 employees)
  2. *Unhappy* (14 employees)
  3. *More money* (11 employees)
* **Strategic Action**:
  * **Stay Interviews & Pulse Checks**: Conduct quarterly stay interviews for Production Technicians approaching their 18-to-24-month tenure milestones (peak voluntary exit window).
  * **Compensation Benchmarking**: Review hourly wages for Production Technicians I and II ($59k–$60k average) against local regional manufacturing benchmarks to mitigate loss of skilled operators to competing employers.

---

## 2. Optimize Recruitment Channel Budget & Strategy

* **Verified Finding**: **Indeed (87 hires)** and **LinkedIn (76 hires)** deliver 52.4% of total recruitment volume. However, **Employee Referrals (31 hires)** and **Diversity Job Fairs (29 hires)** demonstrate strong retention and workforce diversification.
* **Strategic Action**:
  * **Scale Employee Referral Incentives**: Referral hires demonstrate high retention and low onboarding friction. Expand referral bonuses for specialized technical and production roles.
  * **Channel Prioritization**: Reallocate paid search acquisition spend (Google Search: 49 hires) into targeted professional networks (LinkedIn for IT/IS and Engineering) and community diversity partnerships.

---

## 3. Equitable Special Project Allocation & Career Development

* **Verified Finding**: Analysis of `SpecialProjectsCount` across departments (Sheet 12) reveals heavy concentration in **IT/IS (296 projects)**, **Software Engineering (46 projects)**, and **Admin (33 projects)**, while Production employees received only **4 special projects total** (mean: 0.02 per employee).
* **Strategic Action**:
  * **Cross-Functional Stretch Assignments**: Introduce continuous improvement / Lean manufacturing special projects within Production to provide technicians with career development opportunities, improving engagement and retention.

---

## 4. Manager Coaching & Performance Calibration

* **Verified Finding**: Manager cross-tabulations (Sheet 13) show variations in performance ratings and PIP assignments across team leads (e.g., Brannon Miller managing 7 'Exceeds' and 4 'PIP' cases; John Smith with 2 'PIP' cases).
* **Strategic Action**:
  * **Objective Rating Rubrics**: Establish standardized cross-departmental calibration sessions before annual review cycles to eliminate supervisor-level rating bias and support underperforming team members early.
