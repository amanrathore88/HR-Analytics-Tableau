# Project Objective & Business Context

## Executive Summary

The objective of this HR Analytics project is to evaluate organizational workforce dynamics, recruitment channel effectiveness, historical employee attrition, performance distributions, and workplace engagement across a mid-sized organization (311 employees).

Originally developed as an applied Tableau analytics project, this repository preserves the authentic calculations, interactive dashboards, and visual architecture while providing rigorous documentation for talent leaders, HR business partners (HRBPs), and data recruiters.

---

## Core Business Questions Addressed

1. **Workforce Composition & Demographics**:
   * What is the demographic distribution of the workforce across age brackets and gender?
   * How is headcount distributed across key business units (Production, IT/IS, Sales, Software Engineering, Admin, Executive)?
2. **Attrition & Turnover Dynamics**:
   * What are the historical termination volumes and annualized departure patterns?
   * What are the primary stated reasons for voluntary and involuntary employee exits?
3. **Recruitment Channel Effectiveness**:
   * Which recruitment channels (Indeed, LinkedIn, Google Search, Employee Referrals, Diversity Job Fairs) generate the highest candidate volume?
   * What are the hiring trends over multi-year operational cycles?
4. **Performance Management & Talent Distribution**:
   * How are employee performance ratings (`Exceeds`, `Fully Meets`, `Needs Improvement`, `PIP`) distributed across the workforce and individual managers?
   * Who are the top-performing contributors eligible for leadership pipelines?
5. **Engagement, Feedback & Project Allocation**:
   * How do employee engagement survey scores distribute across staff?
   * Which business units actively assign stretch special projects to develop internal talent?
6. **Exploratory Pay Equity & Diversity Inquiries**:
   * Are there noticeable compensation disparities across departments or gender groups?
   * How effectively do various recruiting sources contribute to racial and ethnic diversity?

---

## Target Audience & Stakeholder Value

* **Chief Human Resources Officers (CHROs) & People Operations**: High-level visibility into headcount stability, retention bottlenecks, and strategic workforce planning.
* **Talent Acquisition (TA) Leaders**: Cost-per-hire and channel-yield optimization insights to focus sourcing budgets on high-performing recruitment avenues.
* **Department Managers & HRBPs**: Actionable performance distribution visibility, team engagement metrics, and retention risks within specific functional units.
